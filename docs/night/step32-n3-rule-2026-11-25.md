# Step 32 night N3 — corner covariate diagnostic: rule (written 2026-11-25 ~21:15 KST, before any N3 number). Report-only, no attempt.

Central 21:00 (plan claude/plan-night-2026-11-25.md). May not pick values for a judged run.

**Rows.** Every measured corner minimum of the step 32 top-10 laps, 2023–25, with measured v_min 120–240 km/h, aligned (geometry
curvature peak within 100 m of the measured minimum) and not flat out at the apex. Response y = A2 LOTO baseline error at the corner
(model min within ±40 m − measured, km/h; car = a2Grip(track) + shipped 2025 car, as the step 32 fold baseline). Residual-audit classes
A/B/C/D (same rules, applied to this car's rows with error > +20 km/h) kept as labels only.

**Covariates (from data already held; standardised):**
| covariate | source | expected sign of the coefficient (y = model − measured) |
|---|---|---|
| driven/geometry radius ratio R_xy/R_geom | FastF1 x/y (TI, pinned), circle fit ±50 m; track geometry κ | **−** (a wider real line than the geometry: real car faster) |
| vertical curvature at the apex (crest > 0) | geometry grade, d(grade)/ds over ±40 m | **+** (crest unloads the car; the kernel has no vertical-curvature load term) |
| elevation change entry→apex (Δz over the 100 m before) | geometry grade | none (control; grade is in the kernel) |
| kerb corridor at the apex | step 31 corridor.json (13 tracks; others missing) | **−** (kerbs widen the real line; f_kerb = 0 in the model) |
| geometry radius R_geom | geometry κ | none (control) |
| in a complex (another measured apex within 150 m) | measured corners | **+** (driver compromise in a sequence) |
| entry speed (max measured speed in the 300 m before) | measured trace | **+** (margin at high entry speed) |
| lap position s/L | geometry | none (control) |
Banking: **no source** (the geometry has elevation only, no bank angle); omitted.

**Model.** y ~ covariates + track random intercept (statsmodels MixedLM). LOTO by track: fit on 23 tracks, predict the held track with
the fixed part only. R²_LOTO(M) = 1 − SSE(M)/SSE(null), null = intercept-only mixed model, same folds. Per covariate: all-data
coefficient (95 % CI), single-covariate gain R²_LOTO(null + x), drop-one loss from the full model. Kerb covariate on its 13-track subset.

**Decision rule.** If one covariate gives a single-covariate LOTO R² gain ≥ 0.30 AND its all-data coefficient has the expected sign
(95 % CI not crossing zero), central may open a family tomorrow. Controls (no expected sign) cannot trigger. Otherwise the corner spread
goes to the data-limit protocol.

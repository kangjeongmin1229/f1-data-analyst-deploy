## 5. Guards added (all arms; a variant passes only if every guard passes)
- **G2b trap bias:** held-out trap bias (sim − real at the mapped position, straight peak ±300 m) per class within ±2 km/h with its 95 %
  t-interval over tracks inside ±2 km/h; tracks without a position are reported apart and not in G2b.
- **G-E hybrid:** unit test on every LMH/LMDh lap of the judged cars: LMH front-axle deploy only above its rule speed (190 km/h), LMDh
  rear hybrid only on the rear axle at ≤ 50 kW, Σ flow.dep = deployJ within 0.1 %, deployJ ≤ budget·1.001 (per lap; a stint is a sum
  of laps, so it closes when every lap closes).
- **G-T compute:** page lap compute on 10 WEC events, flag on vs today, median of 5 timings: ≤ +20 %.
- **G-B byte identity:** test that the measured tables (sha256 pins) are unchanged before/after, and every F1, FE and GT3 page lap is
  byte-identical with the flags on and off.

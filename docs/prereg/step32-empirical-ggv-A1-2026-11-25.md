A1: Before the run, compute the 2026 baseline rows (shipped 2026 car, A2-style model stage on base 589b6b3: production zones, real 2026 weather, recharge limit, C5.12 ramp, timeScale, held-out grip = geometric mean of the other 2026 tracks' shipped 2026 grip; wet-quali laps excluded as in 30c; alignment corr ≥ 0.93) and their own spread, borrowing nothing from 2023–25: SD = the SD over tracks of (RMS_odd − RMS_even)/√2, where each track's 120–240 km/h apexes are split alternately in lap order (tracks with < 2 such apexes give no SD term; no further √2 correction, so the SD is conservative); MDE_2026 = 2.80 · SD / √n_2026; the 2026 group is judged only if n_2026 ≥ 16 and MDE_2026 ≤ 3 km/h, otherwise it is report-only.

## Why the v1 geometry rule is replaced (seen before this file)
- The corner-count part of rule v1 fails on a **measured** line too: the Monaco OpenF1 driven line (real cars) gives 13 detected corners
  against 19 official turns. So the detector (|κ| peaks, R < 100 m, 50 m merge) undercounts FE-style numbered kinks; it does not
  measure geometry error. Rule v1 is withdrawn, not tuned.
- Line ÷ official length (0.964–0.987) compares a **racing line** (11 m corridor) with a **centre-line** length. Measured F1 driven lines
  (OpenF1, 26 tracks) give 0.979–0.998, median 0.990; the street circuit Monaco gives 0.979. So the ratio alone cannot separate a wrong
  centre line from a normal racing-line shortcut.
- Report-only audit already run (scripts/sim/tracks/fe-geometry-audit.py, similarity ICP of the official map centre line to the OSM
  raceway ways of the venue): see the status doc. It is repeated in the pipeline run that carries this amendment.

## Geometry check v2 (the centre line against an independent source)
Per circuit, the source of the line and its check:
- official FIA FE map centre line with OSM raceway ways at the venue: **good** if the fit has |scale − 1| ≤ 1 %, median distance ≤ 3 m,
  p95 ≤ 15 m and ≥ 95 % of the map line within 15 m of an OSM way;
- OSM ways matched to the map (Mexico, fe-osm-lines.py): **good** if its stored fitMedianM ≤ 10 m (the map is a fill outline there);
- measured driven line (Monaco, OpenF1 car positions): **good** by source;
- no independent source: **unverified** → report-only.
The racing-line corridor (11 m) is not checked by v2: no cited width source exists for FE circuits (written reason, C2). Changing it
would be a model change with its own pre-registration.

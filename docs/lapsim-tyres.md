# Tyre compound rules: F1, WEC, IMSA (2024–2026)

Compiled 2026-11-08 with WebSearch/WebFetch only. Machine-readable version: `tyre-rules.json`.
"Used" means the source only says what teams ran, so availability is inferred, not stated.

## 1. F1 (Pirelli)

- Pirelli picks **3 dry compounds per race**, labelled hard / medium / soft. 2025 range C1–C6. 2026 range **C1–C5** (C6 dropped), with slightly narrower 18-inch tyres. Intermediate and full wet are always available. [F1.com](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/pirelli-confirm-2026-tyre-compounds-as-f1-gets-set-for-a-new-era-of.6la0zKVsCYwWk9AAISz4Yw)
- Standard weekend: **13 dry sets** per driver. Example, Australia 2026: 2 hard (C3), 3 medium (C4), 8 soft (C5), plus 4 inter and 3 wet sets. Q3 drivers get one extra soft set. A dry GP needs at least two different compounds. [Mercedes](https://www.mercedesamgf1.com/news/what-are-the-tyre-options-for-the-2026-australian-gp)
- Sprint weekend: **12 dry sets**. Example, Canada 2026: 2 hard, 4 medium, 6 soft, plus 6 inter and 3 wet sets. [F1.com](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/what-tyres-will-the-teams-and-drivers-have-for-the-2026-canadian-grand-prix.2Olo6kVcWh1n3FfU0OancF)
- GP qualifying: no mandatory compound. The Q2 start-tyre rule was dropped from 2022, so everyone chooses their starting tyre freely. [Autosport](https://www.autosport.com/f1/news/f1-regulations-update-confirms-removal-of-q2-tyre-rule/8313153/)
- Sprint qualifying (dry): a new medium in SQ1, a new medium in SQ2, and a soft (new or used) in SQ3, one set per stage. Tyre choice is free in the sprint race. [RN365](https://racingnews365.com/new-f1-qualifying-rules-for-2026-explained), [Motor Sport](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/articles/single-seaters/f1/how-2026-f1-sprint-races-work-qualifying-points-and-schedule/)

## 2. WEC Hypercar (Michelin)

**Range:** soft (white), medium (yellow), hard (red) in all three years. 2026 brought a new Pilot Sport Endurance range with the same labels. **Two slicks per round, all three at Le Mans.** [Michelin 2024 kit](https://espacioprensa.michelin.es/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Michelin-FIA-WEC-2024-POR.pdf), [RACER 2025-06-04](https://racer.com/2025/06/04/michelin-gtp-hypercar-tires-get-ready-to-roll)

**Wet:** there is **one wet spec and no intermediate**. Wet tyres are unlimited but can only be used once the track is declared wet. The new 2026 wet tyre made its debut at Barcelona (18 Oct 2026). It is built for wet-to-drying conditions, and Michelin says it "will lack the performance of a full wet". [RACER 2026-10-16](https://racer.com/2026/10/16/michelin-introduces-new-hypercar-rain-tire), [FIA D10 2026](https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/wec_2026_d10_all_sporting_clarifications_28042026.pdf)

**Allocation (dry tyres per car):** FP 12, Hyperpole 4, qualifying + race 18 / 26 / 32 for 6h / 8h / 10h races. Le Mans 2025: FP+Q 24, Hyperpole 12, race 56. Le Mans 2026: Hyperpole 12. [FIA D09 2024](https://www.Fia.com/sites/default/files/wec_2024_d09_all_hyperpole_tyre_specification_16012024.pdf), [D10 2026](https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/wec_2026_d10_all_sporting_clarifications_28042026.pdf), [Le Mans 2025 racecard](https://dgaddcosprod.blob.core.windows.net/b2c-experience-production/attachments/cmc08gq9z077501lu624a1yrr-rececard-2025-wec-r04-lemans.pdf)

**Hyperpole:** in 2024 only the event's softest spec was allowed (the hardest at Qatar). From 2025 the compound is set for each event. Hyperpole tyres are for Hyperpole only and cannot be used in the race. No source found any rule that cars must start the race on their qualifying tyre.

## 3. WEC LMGT3 (Goodyear)

Goodyear nominates **one slick spec per race**, plus the Eagle Wet (blue, unlimited) at every round. There was no soft in any year.

- **2024:** Medium (yellow) at every round. A "Medium Plus" (red) was held back and not raced; it later became the Hard. [RACER 2024](https://racer.com/2024/04/17/how-fia-wec-tire-makers-are-chasing-sustainable-performance), [DSC](https://www.dailysportscar.com/2025/07/11/goodyear-debuts-new-lmgt3-hard-tyre.html)
- **2025:** Medium at Qatar, Imola, Spa, Le Mans and Fuji. **Hard** at São Paulo (its debut), COTA and Bahrain. [Goodyear 2025 media guide](https://news.goodyear.eu/download/86c8270b-6332-433e-8404-b573af244a69/goodyear2025mediaguide.pdf)
- **2026:** **Medium only** at every round, and the Hard is retired. [S365](https://sportscar365.com/?p=139745), [FIAWEC](https://www.fiawec.com/en/news/tyre-partners/8259). Goodyear's own race notes confirm the Medium at [Imola](https://news.goodyear.eu/goodyear-racing-notes--wec-6-hours-of-imola/), [São Paulo](https://news.goodyear.eu/goodyear-racing-notes--wec-6-hours-of-so-paulo/) and [COTA](https://news.goodyear.eu/goodyear-racing-notes--wec-lone-star-le-mans/).
- **2026 allocation:** FP 16, Hyperpole 4, qualifying + race 24 / 32 / 40 for 6h / 8h / 10h (44 dry tyres in total for a 6h race). [FIA D10](https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/wec_2026_d10_all_sporting_clarifications_28042026.pdf)

## 4. IMSA

- **GTP:** uses the same Michelin range as the WEC Hypercars, but IMSA allocations differ. [RACER 2025-06-04](https://racer.com/2025/06/04/michelin-gtp-hypercar-tires-get-ready-to-roll)
  - Daytona 2024: soft + medium, with the soft allowed only from 19:00 Saturday to 08:00 Sunday. [IMSA CB 24-06](https://www.imsa.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/32/2024/01/24/CB-IWSC-24-06-GTP-R24H-Tire-Usage-Notes-012424.pdf)
  - Daytona 2026: soft + medium, with the soft allowed only from 17:00 to 10:00. [RACER](https://racer.com/2026/01/24/how-michelin-makes-the-world-go-round-at-the-rolex-24)
  - The new wet tyre arrives from the December 2026 Daytona test.
  - All other events: **NOT FOUND**. The per-event IMSA Michelin bulletins are listed [here](https://www.imsa.com/competitors/2026-michelin-bulletins/), but their PDFs failed to load or their tables could not be extracted.
- **GTD Pro / GTD:** one slick spec, Pilot Sport Pro GT H1+, which is unchanged in compound for 2026, plus the Pilot Sport GT P2L wet. [IMSA price list 2026](https://www.imsa.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/32/2026/05/20/Michelin-Motorsports-2026-IMSA-WeatherTech-SportsCar-Championship-Tire-Pricing-002-1.pdf), [IMSA](https://imsa.com/news/2026/january/15/sustainability-range-set-to-highlight-michelins-new-gtp-tire).

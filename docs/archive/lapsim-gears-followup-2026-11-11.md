The predictor uses road speed only and learns gear-speed centres from other teams in the same season. A single speed can occur in different gears during braking and acceleration, and teams shift differently. A speed-only classifier cannot represent that direction/history. Sampling around shifts adds ambiguity.

## Short-shifting check

A low-RPM straight-upshift proxy accounts for only 0.024% of total error distance, over 0.103 km of eligible windows. The present evidence does not support short-shifting as the main cause. Driver intent is not measured. The proxy is within 0.75 s of an observed upshift above 250 km/h, RPM below 90% of the same team-season/from-gear median, with at least 3 observed shifts.

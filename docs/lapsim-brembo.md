## Conflicts

- Canada 2025: the race page gives 5.0 g max (T10). The 2025-numbers page says Russell pulled 5.1 g at the penultimate corner.
- Belgium 2025: the race page gives a max pedal load of 157 kg. The 2026 page says T18 in 2025 took 160 kg and 4.7 g.
- Miami 2025: the 2026 page gives the 2025 'first corner' as 2,426 kW, 169 kg, 2.60 s, 117 m. The 2025 page gives T17 as 2,462 kW, 169 kg, 2.81 s, 121 m.
- Brazil 2025: the race page gives max 4.6 g / 139 kg (T1). The 2026 article says T12 in 2025 took 4.7 g / 148 kg.
- Mexico 2025: the race page gives T1 126 kg as the hardest. The 2026 article says T4 in 2025 took 131 kg.
- infernal-circuits (2025 overview): Bahrain is rated maximum (5), but the 2025 Bahrain page says 4. Braking events per lap there (Brazil 5, Monza 6, Las Vegas 6, Singapore 12, Baku 11) differ from the 2025 race pages (7, 7, 7, 11, 12), so the overview probably uses 2024 data.
- Bahrain 2026: 189 kg pedal load at T15 is an outlier against every other 2026 figure (86-123 kg).
